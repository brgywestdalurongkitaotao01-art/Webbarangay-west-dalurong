Public assistance

Barangay Services

Residents may use the request form below to send an inquiry for a barangay document. Submission through this demo website does not yet constitute an official application.

Request a Barangay Certificate

Choose the document you need and provide your contact details. The barangay office must confirm requirements, fees (if any), schedule, and release before a request is considered accepted.

Important: This is a front-end prototype. Requests are not transmitted to or stored by the Barangay Hall yet. Do not submit sensitive personal information until an official secure submission process is established.

Common documents may include:

  • Barangay Clearance
  • Certificate of Residency
  • Certificate of Indigency
  • Other certifications, subject to barangay requirements

The button only creates a local preview; it does not send your information.
